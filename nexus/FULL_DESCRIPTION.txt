[size=5][b]Workbenches+[/b][/size]

[b]Crafting menus that stay readable.[/b]
Every station sorts its recipes the same smart way: craftable items first, then headers by material and biome, armor sets and weapon materials together, upgrade variants right behind their base item. Plus a search field, favorites, category chips, a Dismantle tab, build range control and optional auto repair. Does not change recipes or crafting logic.

[b]Client-side[/b] · Valheim 1.0 · BepInExPack 5.4.2350+. Console players via crossplay cannot load the mod.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]

[b]Sorting (every station)[/b]
[list]
[*]Recipes you can craft now sit at the top, with their own headers.
[*]Below that: headers by material and biome (Wood, Bronze, Iron, Silver, Black Metal, Flametal, ...), in progression order.
[*]Armor sets stay together: chest, helmet, legs, cape. Weapons of one material stay together, ordered by weapon type.
[*]Variants (Blood, Lightning, Frost/Fire, ...) sit directly behind the weapon they are made from. Deep North casts sit behind the item they cast.
[*]Food: Health, Stamina and Eitr headers, strongest food first. Feasts and fishing bait follow the biomes.
[*]The Forge of Potential list is sorted too.
[*]Nothing to set up: there is no sort mode option, one order for everything.
[/list]

[b]Search and favorites[/b]
[list]
[*]Search field at the right end of the tab row. Filters by recipe name or header (type "iron" to see the Iron block).
[*][b]Right-click[/b] a recipe to make it a favorite. Favorites form a block at the top of every list. Saved in BepInEx/config/WorkbenchesPlus_favorites.txt.
[/list]

[b]Biome tags and food values[/b]
[list]
[*]Gear headers carry the biome of their material, for example Iron [Swamp], Black Metal [Plains].
[*]Food shows its strongest bar after the name: Health red, Stamina yellow, Eitr blue.
[/list]

[b]Category chips[/b]
[list]
[*]Chips under the repair button: Weapons, Armor, Shields, Tools, Ammo, Magic, Health, Stamina, Eitr, Feasts, Prep, Fish, Bait, Potions, Cast, Materials, Trinkets, Clothes, Misc. Empty chips are hidden.
[*]Click [b]Weapons[/b] and a small filter panel opens under it: a grid of symbols (1-Hand, 2-Hand, Knives, Swords, Clubs, Axes, Spears, Polearms, Bows, Crossbows, Fists, Bombs). Hand and type combine (2-Hand + Axes). A 1-handed axe is in 1-Hand and in Axes.
[*][b]All[/b] shows recipes for the current station only.
[/list]

[b]Upgrade tab[/b]
[list]
[*]Shows the current stat next to the upgraded one, for example Durability: 400 (now: 200).
[/list]

[b]Dismantle[/b]
[list]
[*]New tab beside Craft / Upgrade: break station-crafted items back into their materials.
[*]Food and potions are never listed. The refund percentage is configurable.
[*]Not available at the Forge of Potential.
[/list]

[b]Craft multiplier[/b]
[list]
[*]Up / down arrows beside the Craft button set how many crafts to run (up to 99).
[/list]

[b]Build range[/b]
[list]
[*]Look at a station the hammer needs (workbench, forge, ...) and press [b]Shift+E[/b] (AltPlace + Use) to cycle its build range: default, 50, 100, 150, 200 m.
[*]Saved on the station. Not possible inside someone else's ward.
[/list]

[b]Auto repair (off by default)[/b]
[list]
[*]Repairs worn armor, weapons and tools one item at a time while you stand inside the build range of a matching station. Needs roof and fire like the normal repair.
[/list]

[b]Note for servers[/b]
[list]
[*]The mod is purely client-side and works on any server. Dismantle, build range and auto repair are decided on your client, so a server with strict rules may not want them. Each can be switched off in the config.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Config[/b][/size]

One file only (no YAML):

[code]BepInEx/config/com.morda.workbenchesplus.cfg[/code]

[list]
[*][b]EnableMod[/b]: Master switch
[*][b]EnableCategories[/b]: Category chips
[*][b]ShowCraftabilityIndicators[/b]: Green check on fully craftable rows (default off)
[*][b]EnableCraftMultiplier[/b]: Multi-craft arrows beside Craft
[*][b]EnableUpgradeCompare[/b]: Upgrade tab: current stat next to the upgraded one
[*][b]EnableDismantle[/b]: Dismantle tab
[*][b]DismantleRefundPercent[/b]: Percent of materials returned (0-100, default 100)
[*][b]EnableBuildRangeCycle[/b]: Shift+E build range cycle
[*][b]BuildRangeMaxMeters[/b]: Highest build range (50-500, default 200)
[*][b]EnableAutoRepair[/b]: Auto repair (default off)
[*][b]DebugLogging[/b]: Writes a recipe dump for bug reports
[/list]
