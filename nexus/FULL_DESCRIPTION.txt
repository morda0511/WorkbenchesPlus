[size=5][b]Workbenches+[/b][/size]

[b]Crafting menus that stay readable.[/b]
Sort and filter station recipes so craftable items rise first, categories sit under the repair button, and armor sets and weapon materials stay together in biome order. Dismantle station-crafted items back into their materials from a tab beside Craft / Upgrade. Does not change recipes or crafting logic.

[b]Client-side[/b] · Valheim 1.0 · BepInExPack 5.4.2350+. Console players via crossplay cannot load the mod.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]

[b]Craftable first[/b]
[list]
[*]Recipes you can craft now sit at the top.
[*]Partial materials next, then the rest.
[*]Uncraftable set pieces stay below craftable ones.
[/list]

[b]Category chips[/b]
[list]
[*]Chips under the repair button: All, Weapons, Armor, Shields, Tools, Building, Furniture, Food, Potions, Materials, Misc.
[*][b]All[/b] shows recipes for the current station only.
[/list]

[b]Armor set grouping[/b]
[list]
[*]Helmet, chest, legs, and cape from the same set stay together.
[*]Piece order is configurable.
[/list]

[b]Weapon material grouping[/b]
[list]
[*]Weapons of the same material stay together (Iron with Iron, Black Metal with Black Metal).
[*]Groups follow biome / material tier order (Copper, Bronze, Iron, …).
[/list]

[b]Sort modes[/b]
[list]
[*]CraftableFirst, CategoryThenCraftable, Progression, Alphabetical, Vanilla.
[/list]

[b]Craft multiplier[/b]
[list]
[*]Up / down arrows beside the Craft button set how many crafts to run.
[*]Caps at MaxCraftMultiplier (default 99).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Config[/b][/size]

One file only (no YAML):

[code]BepInEx/config/com.morda.workbenchesplus.cfg[/code]

[list]
[*][b]EnableMod[/b]: Master switch
[*][b]EnableCategories[/b]: Category chips
[*][b]EnableCraftableFirst[/b]: Prioritize craftable recipes
[*][b]EnableArmorSetGrouping[/b]: Keep armor sets together
[*][b]EnableWeaponSetGrouping[/b]: Keep weapons by material together
[*][b]SortMode[/b]: CraftableFirst / CategoryThenCraftable / Progression / Alphabetical / Vanilla
[*][b]ArmorPieceOrder[/b]: Order inside an armor set
[*][b]WeaponPieceOrder[/b]: Order inside a weapon material group
[*][b]GroupModdedArmorSets[/b]: Heuristics for modded armor
[*][b]GroupModdedWeaponSets[/b]: Heuristics for modded weapons
[*][b]RefreshOnInventoryChange[/b]: Re-sort when inventory changes
[*][b]RefreshOnStationChange[/b]: Reset category to All when switching stations
[*][b]ShowCraftabilityIndicators[/b]: Green check on fully craftable rows
[*][b]EnableCraftMultiplier[/b]: Multi-craft arrows beside Craft
[*][b]MaxCraftMultiplier[/b]: Max multi-craft amount
[*][b]DebugLogging[/b]: Extra logs
[/list]
