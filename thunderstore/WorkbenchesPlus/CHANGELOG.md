# Changelog

## 1.0.12

### UI IMPROVEMENTS
- Black Forge: Gold section renamed to **Bloodgold**
- Black Forge leftovers sorted: Capes, Tools (hook / lantern / shovel / saddles), Materials (Bell), Special (Crown), Fire (Dyrnwyn)

## 1.0.11

### FIX
- Food Preparation Table: Feasts have their own header; Fish is raw fish only; Bait chip last
- Galdr: Cast blanks stay Cast (no Prep)
- Black Forge: Bows and Crossbows are separate groups; named weapons (Berzerkr, Eldner, Slayer, …) get their own labels; Gold variants stay under Gold

## 1.0.10

### FIX
- Galdr / Black Forge: Deep North `*Uncooked` blanks are **Cast**, not Prep (Prep chip no longer appears there)
- Category chips stay station-only — foreign categories are not shown

## 1.0.9

### NEW FEATURES
- Galdr Table: **Cast** chip for Deep North moulds (Gussformen)
- Food Preparation Table: own chips — Feasts, Prep, Bait, Fish

### UI IMPROVEMENTS
- Cauldron Health / Stamina / Eitr lists sort by food value (highest first)

## 1.0.8

### NEW FEATURES
- Cauldron / oven / mead ketill: category chips for **Health**, **Stamina**, and **Eitr** (from vanilla food bars, or mead name)

### UI IMPROVEMENTS
- Food list under All is grouped by Health / Stamina / Eitr section labels

## 1.0.7

### NEW FEATURES
- Each crafting station has its own category chips (cauldron: Food / Feasts / Bait; mead ketill: Potions; galdr: Magic; forge: Ammo / Trinkets; …)

### UI IMPROVEMENTS
- Arrow and bolt recipes use an Ammo chip instead of Weapons
- Fishing baits and feasts have their own chips at the cauldron

## 1.0.6

### FIX
- Forge of Potential: upgrade list is left to vanilla (no category filter or re-sort), so items show again

## 1.0.5

### FIX
- Cauldron / cooking: fishing baits sit under Food (Bait), not Weapons / Arrows
- Mead bases stay with Potions, and food recipes no longer pick up weapon material labels (Wolf, Bone, …)

## 1.0.4

### NEW FEATURES
- Look at a workbench (or other station with a build radius) and press **Shift+E** to add 50 m of build range, up to +200 m, then back to default. Saved on that station.

## 1.0.3

### FIX
- Forge of Potential: upgrade list and sorting work again (the station filter no longer hides every recipe)
